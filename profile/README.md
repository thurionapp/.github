## 💡 What is Thurion?

Thurion is an innovative app that transforms your vehicle into a **Living Entity**: it understands how your car is being used, identifies wear patterns, and predicts when maintenance is needed.

### What Thurion is NOT
* ❌ A traditional GPS tracker
* ❌ A driver surveillance system  
* ❌ An app for control or punishment

### What Thurion IS
* ✅ A vehicle health system
* ✅ A predictive maintenance assistant
* ✅ An intelligent mileage tracker
* ✅ An economy and predictability tool
